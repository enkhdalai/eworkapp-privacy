# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 3, 2026

Eworkmapp is operated by Enkhdalai Erdenebat behalf of Bank of Mongolia (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Eworkmapp mobile application.

## Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

- Account information, such as your name, work email address, phone number, employee ID, username, and role.
- Employment-related information required to provide app services, such as attendance, requests, internal letters, assigned assets, or workplace-service activity.
- Location information when you choose to allow location access. Location is used only to provide location-based features, such as showing your position on a map.
- Information you submit through the app, including requests, comments, forms, and attachments.
- Technical information needed to operate and secure the app, such as device type, operating-system version, app version, IP address, authentication tokens, and security logs.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Authenticate users and provide authorized access to app services.
- Deliver workplace and employee services.
- Display relevant employee, attendance, request, asset, and internal-service information.
- Maintain security, prevent unauthorized access, investigate technical issues, and comply with legal obligations.
- Improve the reliability and performance of the app.

## Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information only with:

- Authorized employees and internal systems required to provide the app’s services.
- Approved service providers that support app operation, security, hosting, maps, or notifications.
- Government authorities or other parties when required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

## Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the app’s services, meet operational requirements, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.
