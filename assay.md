# Assay Center (Сорьцын төв) Customer App Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

The Assay Center customer app lets you view the results for bullion you submitted, check its certificate, and choose a commercial bank for a signed bullion item. This policy explains how Assay Center handles your information when you use the Android app.

## Signing in

When you enter your phone number or registration number, we use it to find your existing customer record. We send the phone number on that record to WeSend, our SMS verification provider. WeSend delivers a one-time code and checks the code you enter against a verification reference. The code expires after five minutes.

After you sign in, the app saves a session token in secure storage on your device. The service keeps a hashed copy of the token, your customer ID, an app label, and session timestamps to authenticate requests and limit access to your records. The app connects to the service over HTTPS. A session expires after 30 days. You can remove the token from your device at any time by selecting **Гарах** (Sign out) in your profile.

## Your bullion and certificates

The app retrieves records that your assay center already holds about your submissions. These include the bullion number, receiving center, date received, weight, and gold and silver test results. For a certificate, the app can also show its number, issue date, verification reference, digital signature status, signing date, and a digital fingerprint of the document. We use these details to show which results belong to your submission and whether its certificate has been signed.

## Who can see your information

When you assign a signed bullion item to a commercial bank, we record the item, certificate, bank, your customer identifier, and the time of your choice. Authorized staff at that bank can review the assigned record, including the customer name, customer type, registration number, bullion number, weight, assay results, and certificate details. If the certificate covers several bullion items, its signed contents can include details of those items as well. You can choose another bank for the item. We keep a history of assignments and changes.

Authorized assay center staff handle customer and assay records as part of receiving bullion and issuing certificates. Authorized Bank of Mongolia staff can review published, signed bullion records across centers, independently of your commercial bank selection. Providers that host the service and its database process records to operate the app. WeSend processes the phone number and verification information for sign-in. We may also disclose records when required by law.

## Retention and deletion

Signing out removes your saved sign-in token from that device. Your assay center keeps the underlying customer, bullion, certificate, and bank assignment records for as long as needed to verify issued results, document who received access, resolve disputes, and meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. Expiration of a code or session ends its use for sign-in. It does not erase the assay records.

## Privacy questions and requests

To ask what information we hold, correct a customer record, or request deletion of your mobile access and associated personal information, email [info@ardni-labs.com](mailto:info@ardni-labs.com). Identify the submission by its bullion or certificate number if available. Please keep your SMS sign-in code private. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. If an official record must be retained, you can ask which information is kept and why.
