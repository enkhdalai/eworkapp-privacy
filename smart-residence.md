# Smart Residence Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 4, 2026

Smart Residence is a residential-services application provided for residents by the relevant property-management organization (the “Organization”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Smart Residence mobile app, Apple Watch app, and related resident services.

## Information We Handle

Depending on the services available for your residence and the features you use, we handle:

- **Account and residence information:** your name, phone number, resident account ID, apartment or unit assignment, and household vehicle registration details.
- **Billing information:** monthly invoices, payment amounts, payment status, prior balances, eBarimt-related invoice information, and payment history.
- **Notification information:** the device token used to send push notifications, notification preferences, and delivery status.
- **Maintenance information:** maintenance descriptions and photos uploaded by an authorized property administrator.
- **Gate-access location:** when you request to open a gate, the app requests your precise location solely to confirm that you are within the permitted 50-metre area. The location is used for that immediate validation and is not retained as location history.
- **Technical and security information:** authentication sessions, app and device information needed to operate the service securely, and security or audit records for important actions such as gate commands and invoice administration.

## Face ID and Other Device Biometrics

Smart Residence can use Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode to make sign-in easier. Biometric templates never leave your device. The app receives only the success or failure result supplied by Apple’s device authentication system.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- authenticate residents and provide access to their residence’s services;
- show invoices, payment history, eBarimt information, notices, maintenance updates, and resident reports;
- send push notifications selected by the resident or sent by an authorized property administrator;
- allow an authorized resident to request gate access while within the configured location boundary;
- prevent unauthorized access, protect the service, and maintain an audit trail for administrative and gate-control actions; and
- meet applicable legal, accounting, and operational requirements.

## Payments

Payments are processed by QPay. Smart Residence may provide QPay with invoice information needed to create or display a payment request. We do not collect or store your bank-card number, bank-account number, or QPay credentials. QPay’s handling of payment information is governed by its own privacy practices.

## When Information Is Shared

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Information may be shared only with:

- the Organization and its authorized administrators, to operate the residential services for your property;
- service providers that support the app, such as secure hosting, database, notification, and payment-request providers;
- other residents only where the app intentionally publishes property-level information, such as an apartment number listed in a payment-status report; and
- authorities or other parties where disclosure is required by law or a valid legal request.

## Data Retention

We retain account, invoice, payment, and administrative records for as long as needed to operate the services, satisfy accounting and legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Push-notification tokens are removed when you disable notifications or when the notification provider reports that a token is no longer valid. Gate-location data is not retained after the immediate access check.

## Your Choices

You can:

- enable or disable push notifications from the app or device settings;
- enable or disable Face ID or Touch ID from your profile settings;
- disable location permission in device settings. Gate access will not be available while permission is disabled or while your location cannot be verified; and
- request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to legal and accounting retention obligations.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including authenticated access, encrypted network connections, access controls, and audit logging for sensitive operations. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children’s Privacy

Smart Residence is intended for authorized residents and property administrators. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.
